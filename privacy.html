Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Beyond the Known is a creator-operated video review and publishing workflow.

Information We Process

When a TikTok account is authorized, the application may process account identifiers, OAuth credentials, and video publishing data necessary to perform the features the account owner authorizes.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to authenticate the authorized account, upload approved videos, and check publishing status.

Storage and Security

Authentication credentials are intended to be stored in restricted local application storage and are not published on this website.

Third Parties

Publishing operations communicate with TikTok's official services. TikTok processes information according to its own privacy policy.

Data Deletion and Contact

Account owners may request disconnection and deletion of locally retained account credentials and associated publishing records by contacting the application operator through the contact channel published on this website.
